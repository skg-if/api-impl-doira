### mEDRA product type vocabulary

`product_type` is hard-coded to `literature` for every record - `MedraOnixXmlParser`
only recognizes the ONIX-for-DOI "Serial Article" schema (the only variant seen live, across
both its `WorkRegistrationMessage` and `VersionRegistrationMessage` root-element forms - see
[Known limitations](SKG_IF_DOI_MAPPING_LIMITATIONS.md)); any other ONIX-DOI schema family (Book,
Monograph Chapter, Conference Proceeding, Dissertation) isn't parsed at all and the request 404s
rather than guessing a `product_type` for it. There is no routed-to-Grants case for mEDRA - see
[Product ↔ Grant routing](SKG_IF_DOI_MAPPING_GRANT.md#product--grant-routing).
